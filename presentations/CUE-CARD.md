# Ignite cue card

Print this. Speaker notes are invisible without a second screen, which is the
normal case on an Ignite stage. One line per slide, in order. Slides advance
every 15 seconds whether or not you are ready.

**Quarter marks:** slide 5 at 1:15, slide 10 at 2:30, slide 15 at 3:45.
If the number on screen is ahead of the line you are on, skip forward. Never
back up.

## The three that go missing under pressure

1. **Slide 7 is silent.** Fifteen seconds, no words. It is the hardest thing on
   the card and the easiest to talk over.
2. **Slide 10's compaction beat.** "It compacted, forgot its own rules, said
   you're absolutely right." Slide 13 lists amnesia and sycophancy as
   contributing factors, so if this is skipped the audience reads two items that
   were never set up.
3. **Slide 12 ends in silence too.** Nine seconds of speech, six of nothing.

## Where to claw back time if you are behind

Slides 2 and 3. The setup is the part that stretches: the FedEx run, the stir
fry, "it'll be fine". Every second there is a second not spent on the guardrails
the talk exists to deliver.
